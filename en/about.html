About us

The story behind every cacao bean.

Origin

From red soil, an inspiration took root.

Some lands do more than grow crops — they leave their mark on every ingredient. For Bazan Cacao, the inspiration began with red basalt soil: a place of sun, wind and the untapped potential of Vietnamese produce.

We saw in cacao a value worth discovering and retelling from a new angle. Bazan Cacao was born from that inspiration.

Philosophy

Honour the original, uncover its nuances.

A cacao pod holds more than we usually think: from the rich-tasting bean to the softly fragrant husk, every part has its own potential.

Rather than limiting cacao to one familiar way of enjoying it, Bazan Cacao makes pure cacao powder, 85% dark chocolate and cacao husk tea. Each has its own character, and each keeps the essence of cacao.

Experience

So Vietnamese cacao can be enjoyed in more ways.

The value of cacao lies not only in the finished product, but in what people discover as they enjoy it: a cup of pure cacao, a rich piece of chocolate, or a workshop where you make something of your own.

Bazan Cacao hopes cacao becomes a familiar part of every day, and spreads appreciation for the gifts of Vietnam's nature.

The life of a cacao pod

One cacao pod, more than one product.

  1. Cacao beans

    Sourced from the red basalt soil region.

  2. Fermenting and drying

    The beans are fermented and dried in the sun and wind to keep their bright acidity.

  3. Roasting and shelling

    The beans become cacao powder and chocolate; the husk is kept.

  4. Husk into tea

    The part usually thrown away becomes cacao husk tea.

Mission

To awaken the value of every Vietnamese cacao pod: the beans become pure, low-sugar products and the husk becomes a fragrant cup of tea, so that cacao is not just something to eat but a source of pride carried far.

Vision

By 2030, to be Vietnam's leading green cacao brand, bringing cacao from red basalt soil to Asia, Europe and North America, while helping protect the forests that nurture its taste.

Core values

Original

Natural taste, nothing mixed in.

Sustainable

Using by-products, limiting waste.

Wholesome

Less sugar, clear ingredients.

Raising Vietnamese produce

Creating more value for Vietnamese agriculture.

Our quality promise

Great taste is only the beginning.

  • Pure: real cacao, never diluted or replaced with substitutes.
  • Less sugar: sugar kept to a minimum to preserve cacao's natural taste.
  • Transparent: ingredients, origin and directions printed in full on the pack.
  • Certified: fully registered products, made in a food-safety certified facility.
