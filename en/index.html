About Bazan Cacao

From red basalt soil, a Vietnamese cacao.

Bazan Cacao begins with cacao beans grown under the sun and wind, on mineral-rich red soil that gives them a deep flavour with a bright, gentle acidity. We keep that original taste in every product, hoping to bring Vietnamese cacao closer to everyday life — and to the world.
